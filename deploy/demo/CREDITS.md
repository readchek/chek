# Demo library credits

Everything the seed downloads for the demo's Comics and Books libraries, with its source and license. [`library.list`](library.list) pins each file by SHA-256. The corpus files the libraries also use (Pepper&Carrot, Little Nemo and the EPUB samples) are credited in the `provenance` folder of the Test corpus.

Every author, illustrator and painter of the public-domain works below died before 1956, so those works are public domain in the United States and in countries with terms of life plus 70 years. Covers whose painter died later, such as Standard Ebooks' *The Great Gatsby* and *The Wind in the Willows*, were left out for that reason.
