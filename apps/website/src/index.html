Where your
books live.

Use the files on your device or connect a library server. Both are part of the plan for Chek.

Local files

Open your own books and comics on your device. You won’t need to run a server to use the reader.

Komga and Kavita

Connect the reader to your preferred open-source library service. Komga and Kavita are planned sources.

Chek server

A standalone, open-source library server is also in development. It will be another source you can choose, alongside local files and other providers.
