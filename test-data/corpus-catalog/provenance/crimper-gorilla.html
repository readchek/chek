About the cover

When I first published this as an ebook, I used an excellent photo by Dominik Bartsch on the cover. At the time I obtained it (2015), it was licensed under CC:BY, which only required acknowledgment. Recently I discovered that the license was changed on 4 June 2020 to cc:By-NC-ND (Acknowledgement, Non-Commercial, No-Derivatives) which is much more restrictive and, under that licence I am not permitted to use it. I am not sure where I stand legally here, as the licence under which I first obtained the image permitted my use of it and thus I could possibly still use it under that. I am not going to though. I do not wish to go against the photographers wishes and so I now created my own cover from scratch.

A second edition of this book used an alternative photograph. I now include that in this book on the website as an illustration. Under the public domain license it is released under, I am not even obliged to acknowledge the photographer but it is such a good image that I feel remiss not doing so. So the excellent photo I have used is “Gorilla Portrait” by Petr Kratochvil licensed under CC:0 (Public Domain). I have done considerable research to find if this image truly is under public domain and nothing tells me that it isn’t. If you believe that to be untrue, please do let me know with any evidence you have and I will remove it.

About the formats

The download links above are in various formats. Some of them may be new so here's some more info about them. If you are planning on editing them, please have a look at the licensing on the Legal page.

Depending on the way your system is setup, some of the files may open directly in your browser so you may need to use right click or a menu option to download it (e.g. "Save link as").

PDF

Formatted text. Includes images and covers. Not editable. Requires PDF reader software such as Adobe Reader or X-Reader . To be honest most web browsers will read PDFs these days. Good, simple choice for desktop or laptop devices.

ePub

Formatted text with a cover image. Requires compatible ePub reader software/Ebook reader device . Good choice for mobile devices compatible apps available for most platforms. Can be edited with suitable software such as Calibre

Fb2

Formatted text with cover image. Requires compatible fb2 reader software (such as fbreader . Another good choice for mobile devices. Reader software available for most platforms. Can be edited with suitable software such as Calibre

ODT - Open Document Text document

Formatted, editable text. No images at all. Suitable for word processing software and can be edited in them. Default format for Libre Office . A plugin may be required for some wordprocessors (such as MS Word)

Plain text

Also known as ASCII. Unformatted text. No images at all. Can be read in the widest range of software , on pretty much any device and in browsers.

SVG - Scalable Vector Graphics

This is an image format and will be used for things like bookmarks. It is editable and will require some suitable vector graphics software such as Inkscape on your PC or laptop.

What do you think?

Do you like this book? Yes or No, I'd like to ask you a favour? Would you write a review of it? The review could be on your own blog (or maybe as a guest post on a parent's one), in a school newspaper or on a school's pupil's website.

Why do I want you to write a review? Because I want people to read the book (I'm really more interested in readers than sales although if you want to add a review on Amazon that would be nice) and also because I'd like to know what people think of it.

That way I can work out whether what to change in any future books (or even whether to write any future books). If you do write one, I'd love to read it so please do let me know .
